# Sources

Where entries in this guide were found. The source for each product is in [products.csv](products.csv). This page lists the documents that were mined and what each one is good for. All sources were checked on 2026-11-27 unless noted.

## Industry publications

- [ICF Builder Magazine product directory](https://www.icfmag.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/ICF-Product-Directory.pdf): the current (2025) directory. The 2017 path is an artifact of where the file was first uploaded; it's replaced each year.
- [ICF Builder Magazine 2026 issues](https://www.icfmag.com/pdf_dl/2026/): six issues, January through November. Ads, news and columns.
- [ICF Builder bracing chart](https://www.icfmag.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/ICF-Bracing-Chart.pdf) and [ICF comparison chart](https://icfmag.com/icf-comparison-chart/): side-by-side data still to be verified.

## Manufacturer-published compatible-product lists

These are the strongest leads. A wall maker names a third-party product because its own details and support depend on it, not because someone paid for the listing. A product named by several competing manufacturers is a stronger signal than any single listing.

- [Pow-R-Wall technical library](https://www.powrwall.com/resources/technical-library): "ICF trade partner directory" covering connectors, bucks, bracing, floors, footings, stucco, termite barriers, reinforcement and fasteners.
- [Element ICF Supporting Products, rev. 2025-10](https://elementicf.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Supporting-Products-100725.pdf), from the [Element technical library](https://elementicf.com/technical-library/): finishes, membranes, drainage, sealants, tapes, bracing and vibrators. Also fiberglass-rebar compatibility letters and floor/roof details. The PDF lays products and makers out in two columns that don't line up, so pairings not confirmed elsewhere are left out of the guide.
- [Alleguard ICF](https://alleguard.com/products/construction/icf/) and its [resource library](https://alleguard.com/resource-library/).
- Nudura: [below-grade waterproofing](https://www.nudura.com/below-grade-waterproofing-with-nudura-icf), [weather barrier](https://www.nudura.com/weather-resistant-barrier-with-nudura-icf), [flashing](https://www.nudura.com/tremco-flashing-solutions-with-nudura-icf), [cladding](https://www.nudura.com/cladding-solutions-for-nudura-icf) and [products](https://www.nudura.com/products). Third-party products are mostly from Tremco sister brands.
- Quad-Lock: [accessories](https://www.quadlock.com/insulated-concrete-forms/accessories.htm), [product catalogue](https://www.quadlock.com/brochures/Quad-Lock-Product-Catalog.pdf) and [technical library](https://www.quadlock.com/technical_library/).
- [TF Forming detail manual](https://www.tfsystem.com/detail-manual/) and [FAQ](https://www.tfsystem.com/faqs/).
- [IntegraSpec installation manual, section 19](https://integraspec.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Integraspec-Installation-Manual-v-9.1-with-catalogue.pdf): EPS compatibility and drainage requirements for waterproofing.
- [Stronghold resources](https://www.strongholdicf.com/resources/), [SuperForm accessories](https://www.superformicf.com/accessories/), [LiteForm accessories](https://liteform.com/products/accessories.html) and the [IntegraSpec catalogue](https://integraspec.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Integraspec-Catalogue-Eng_23.pdf).
- [BuildBlock store](https://store.buildblock.com/) and [how-to guides](https://buildblock.com/how-to-build-an-icf-home/).
- Fox Blocks: product and accessory pages only. Bulletin 1.15.01 (Compatible Products) on [foxblocks.com/resources](https://www.foxblocks.com/resources) is behind a contact form and has not been reviewed.

## Primary research and references

- [RDH Building Science ICF field testing report](https://www.bchousing.org/publications/ICF-Field-Test-Report.pdf): basis for ["NoBuck"](research/nobuck.md).
- [HUD, Insulating Concrete Forms for Residential Construction](https://www.huduser.gov/portal/Publications/PDF/icf_res.pdf): prescriptive design guide.
- [Portland Cement Association](https://www.cement.org/): ICF technical resources.
- [ICF Manufacturers Association](https://icf-ma.org/about-the-icfma/primary-members/): member list for cross-checking brands and parent companies.
