# ICF waterproofing

**Use coatings, primers, adhesives and membranes explicitly compatible with EPS. Solvent-based formulations can attack the foam.** Compatibility is product-specific; do not assume that every asphalt membrane is incompatible or every water-based product is approved.

Below grade, distinguish dampproofing from waterproofing for hydrostatic exposure. Coordinate the membrane, protection/drainage layer, footing drains, penetrations and backfill with the form manufacturer's details.
