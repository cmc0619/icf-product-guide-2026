# ICF product guide

Interesting stuff I found for building with ICF.

An independent, evolving construction knowledge base. Entries are organized by function, regardless of discovery source. Thanks to [ICF Magazine](https://www.icfmag.com/) for its [product directory](https://www.icfmag.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/ICF-Product-Directory.pdf) and to the manufacturers and other sources linked below. No affiliation or endorsement implied. Product descriptions are concise research notes, not independently tested specifications.
